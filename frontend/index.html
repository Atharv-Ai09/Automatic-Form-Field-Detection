    How it works

    1. Preprocessing The page becomes grayscale and noise is reduced.
    2. Candidate detection OpenCV finds text-like regions and gives each a bounding box.
    3. Classification A neural network trained on FUNSD labels each region as question, answer, header or other.
    4. Result Boxes and labels are drawn on the image. Confidence is the network's own probability.
